# Executive recommendation

**Make the fixed THINK+mixer–dynamic rank ensemble the subject of one controlled information-versus-exposure study. Start with saved predictions; do not train another model yet.** Retain ten seeds and the primary top 10 portfolio. Test whether the dynamic scores add net selection value over the parent and a causal persistent-stock substitute, then freeze a prospective comparison if the artifact and attribution gates pass.

Rishi's latest work deserves more than dismissal: the primary blend reports net Sharpe **0.755** and CAGR **23.92%**; the secondary top-five, ten-seed version reports **0.896 / 31.87%** and remains positive at four times modeled costs. The selected **0.919** leader uses the original five seeds and five holdings. Its underlying new daily paths and per-stock forecasts remain private, the primary advantage is inconclusive after within-study comparison adjustment, and no matched ablation identifies dynamic membership as the cause. These are coherent historical candidates, not established alpha.
