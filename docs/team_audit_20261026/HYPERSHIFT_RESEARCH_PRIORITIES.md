## Why the leader beats the alternatives

The new blend is sufficiently documented and cost-resilient to merit a focused test. The important uncertainty is whether the added selections reflect time-varying information, a stable stock tilt, or configuration luck. Existing predictions can distinguish some of these explanations without 240 new fits or another sample release. The outcome can close the candidate, simplify it to a transparent control, or justify one frozen prospective test.

Fundamentals have a cleaner information story and lower turnover, but the actual candidate loses to EW and the observed signal is concentrated toward avoiding losers. Testing a newly designed avoidance rule would create a new hypothesis, not validate the failed old book. It does not currently outrank a cheaper audit of the stronger economic candidate.

Conditional modeling is attractive as a paper topic, but conditional high Sharpe cannot be manufactured from the same repeatedly examined four years. Root alignment has an implementation-evidence gap. Intraday work has an execution-evidence gap. Both contributors can help the primary program through tests of geometry/identity and execution, without independently launching another neural architecture.

## Decision-changing outcomes

- If independent replay fails materially, stop interpreting the published candidate until reconciled under a dated amendment.
- If a causal persistent-identity blend matches the dynamic blend within a predeclared economic equivalence margin, simplify the research direction to transparent low-turnover exposure and close the dynamic-alpha story.
- If saved-artifact diagnostics leave credible residual tail information, freeze the existing ten-seed/top 10 blend and controls for new dates. This is permission to observe, not evidence of alpha.
- If future paired net gains survive costs, uncertainty and exposures, continue and then test dynamic membership with a matched static/no-graph design.
- If future effects are meaningfully small or negative, publish the identification/instability result and pivot; do not select a new historical k or regime.

No guaranteed or forecast net SR is assigned to any direction. Expected information value, not the largest historical Sharpe, determines this ranking.
