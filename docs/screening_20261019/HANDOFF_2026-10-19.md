## Caveats to keep in every write-up

- **Survivorship bias:** 2026 constituents are traced back to 2015, so absolute returns and Sharpe are inflated. Comparisons between arms are like-for-like.
- **Sector labels** are current, not point-in-time.
- **Trading is optimistic:** signal-close entry, no delisting payouts.
- **Nothing here is confirmatory.** These years have been inspected before.
- Mechanisms are **simplified adaptations**, not paper reproductions. In particular:
  - ListNet ≠ LapSum;
  - pooled fusion ≠ TimeMixer;
  - masked reconstruction ≠ CI-STHPAN;
  - adaptive normalisation ≠ DoubleAdapt;
  - the MASTER-inspired arm is not MASTER.
- Results do not transfer automatically to Rishi's `feat-rs` model or data, which are still unavailable.
