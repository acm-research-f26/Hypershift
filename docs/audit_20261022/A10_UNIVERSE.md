## Cohort sizes on 1 July of each BC year

- **Index members** = point-in-time S&P 500 on the first listed date ≥ 1 July.
- **C2** = members that are also among the 462 current-constituent survivors (direct symbol match after normalising "." to "-").
- **Survivor share** = C2 / members: the fraction of the historical index the backcast can see. This is a **lower bound**: members that later renamed are not matched by symbol, which also inflates the last column.

## Limits that cannot be removed with free data

- **No delisting returns.** Stocks that left the index or failed are absent: survivor share above.
- **Renamed companies** need a symbol-history map. Without it, C2 under-counts members that later renamed.
- **yfinance history under a current symbol** belongs to the current listing entity and its predecessor, as Yahoo maps it. Spin-offs and mergers can splice histories; this cannot be audited without a corporate-action database.
- **Index weights and float** are not available.
