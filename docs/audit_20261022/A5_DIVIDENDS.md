## T5 verdict (predeclared): **MATERIAL**

- **Criterion 1 (rank distortion): not met.** The median per-date Spearman between adjusted and corrected `log_dvol_20` is 0.995 (5th percentile 0.990), and for `amihud_20` it is 0.996. Ranks are almost unchanged.
- **Criterion 2 (the leak term predicts): met.** The leak term scores +0.0178 [+0.0073, +0.0285]. The part of it that trailing yield cannot explain scores **+0.0305 [+0.0242, +0.0373], positive in 5/5 folds**. That is more than twice the best FB model. Ex-ante trailing yield on its own scores −0.0086 (CI includes 0). The future-only adjustment therefore carries genuine look-ahead information about factor-neutral 5-day returns. It is not a proxy for dividend yield.
- **Criterion 3 (conclusions move by > 0.005): met.**
  - residual − raw: +0.0140 → +0.0089 (CI now touches 0, 4/5 folds).
  - rich − price: −0.0088 (0/5 folds) → −0.0028 (1/5).

### Mechanism: why the leak term predicts returns

- **The factor depends on future prices.** The back-adjustment factor at t is F(t) = ∏ over later ex-dates e of (1 − D_e / P_{e−1}). Each term divides a future dividend by a **future price**.
- **So the 5-day return feeds the factor.** A stock whose price rises after t, including over the 5-day label window, has smaller future D/P ratios. Its L = log F(t) then sits closer to 0.
- **L is therefore partly a function of the label.** It is not only a function of the future dividend policy. This is an exact algebraic channel and needs no economic story.
- **Size of the channel.** The sensitivity is dL/dlog P ≈ cumulative future yield, about 0.1–0.4 over 2019–2023. A 5% move therefore shifts L by about 0.005–0.02, against a cross-sectional SD of L of about 0.05–0.11. That is small next to the spread of `log_dvol_20` (rank correlation 0.995), but strongly predictive once isolated: +0.031.

### Reading

- **The residual-target model does not depend on the leak.** Corrected it scores +0.0136, against +0.0127 originally. With no volume inputs at all (`lgbm:price:residual`) it scores +0.0131.
- **The raw-target LightGBM was *hurt* by the contaminated features.** It moves from −0.0012 to +0.0048, so part of the reported residual − raw gain was the raw model's handicap.
- **FB's "volume features nominally harmful (0/5 folds)" is an artefact of contamination.** Corrected, rich − price is −0.0028, with equivalence within ±0.008. Volume features neither help nor hurt measurably.
- **The leak contaminates three further places in FB:**
  - the `log_dvol_20` regressor of the residual target (label construction, not a model input; it removes the leak-correlated component from labels);
  - the `full` exposure set;
  - the subgroup terciles.

### Prospective pipeline

- **Mechanism.** The v1 scorer will refetch `auto_adjust=True` data at scoring time, about 2027-10.
  - Scoring-date `log_dvol_20` and `amihud_20` will then carry the adjustment for distributions paid between each scoring date and the fetch date, a window of up to about 1 year.
  - Over that window the cross-sectional SD of L is about 0.02–0.05 (the 2023 row above, 3 years before the fetch, is 0.053).
- **Effect on the frozen model.** The frozen model was trained on 2015–2026 features that carry up to 11 years of future adjustment, so it may have learned to use the leak.
- **Bias.** The scoring-window leak is the same kind of look-ahead, and it is not removable after the fact without unadjusted data. Its bias is therefore **upward** on the frozen model's prospective neutral RankIC. reversal16 is unaffected.
