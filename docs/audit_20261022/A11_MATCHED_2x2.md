# A11 — Matched 2×2: feature information × training target (closure review)

Exploratory: the 2019–2023 development years had already been inspected. All inputs are leak-free.

**Design.** One learner throughout: LightGBM (FB `LGBM_PARAMS`), 50 fixed rounds, no validation step. Every cell shares seeds {7, 19, 42}, the FB training window, the evaluation dates and the preprocessing (per-date cs_z plus sector one-hot).

**Cells (features × target):**
- `full` = all 20 FB features + sector one-hot.
- `style` = beta, log_dvol_20, ret_60, vol_60 + sector one-hot. These columns are a subset of `full`.
- `residual` = the FB residual target. `neutral` = the FB evaluation target.

**Statistics.** Primary metric: FB neutral RankIC. Series are seed-averaged daily, pooled as the mean of the 5 year means, with a stationary-bootstrap 95% CI (block 10).

Generated by `scripts/closure_matched.py`.

## Original comparison (verified from code)

In `bcreview_style.py` / A7 the comparison was not matched:
- **the placebo:** `fit_fixed` with 50 rounds, seed 7, **neutral** target, inputs = the four styles + sector one-hot;
- **the "full" model:** the corrected FB early-stopped residual LightGBM, i.e. **residual** target, rounds chosen on validation per year, 20 features, **no** sector one-hot.

Target, rounds and feature nesting all differed. The A7 "model − placebo" contrast is therefore confounded and is superseded by this file.
