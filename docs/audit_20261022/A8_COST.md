# A8 — Cost-aware market-neutral book (predeclared D4)

BC pre-execution review, exploratory (2019–2023 inspected), leak-free predictions.

**Book.** Long the top 10% and short the bottom 10% within each sector (0.5 gross per leg), beta-hedged ex ante with an equal-weight basket of eligible stocks. Weights drift between trades, and hedge trades are costed.

**Schedules:**
- `daily`: rebalance every session;
- `weekly`: rebalance every 5 sessions;
- `weekly_buffer`: weekly, entering at the top/bottom 10% of the sector and exiting outside the top/bottom 30%.

**Execution.** The signal is computed at close t. `close1` trades at close t+1; `open1` trades at open t+1.

**Costs.** c bp per unit of one-way traded notional / NAV. Returns are actual close/open price returns of the implementable book, not residual labels. Annualised returns are arithmetic.
