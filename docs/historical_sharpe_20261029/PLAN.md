2026-10-29. User explicitly requests exploratory optimization of the already-inspected 2022–2025 NDX history, including training approaches. The aim is to measure both the strongest observed historical result and the distribution across seeds and years. ## Study tracks

1. **Blend:** use saved parent and dynamic seed-family ranks. Replay fixed dynamic weights 0, .25, .5, .75, 1 for original-five, new-five, and ten-seed groups; issuer-five/20% risk and top-ten/full, with source 50:50 replay as a parity gate. Dynamic disagreement/volatility switches require a separately frozen subsequent protocol with clocks explicitly defined.
2. **Selection and turnover:** freeze holding counts, rank-weighting and retention thresholds in a separate protocol. Check issuer/share-class ties, past-only state, eligibility, caps, cash, turnover and terminal accounting; compare with matching original target policy.
3. **Risk:** reuse existing 305-recipe study and 525-account attribution; predeclare any additional smoothing and concentration controls, clocks and baseline before scoring. Report return and drawdown along with Sharpe.
4. **Hedging:** reproduce source continuous hedge and compare predeclared hedge sizes, costs and borrowing assumptions with unhedged baseline; use signed share engine and verify against independent account implementation.
5. **Ranking loss:** prepare a matched training design contrasting existing loss with pairwise or top-weighted ranking loss, with same folds, network, data, optimizer budget and seed list; validate on synthetic planted ranking problems before financial fits. Explicitly label new training as exploratory and do not claim original-checkpoint intervention parity.
6. **Cost/risk-aware loss:** validate differentiable turnover and risk objectives on synthetic portfolios including no-lookahead and zero-cost edge cases. Freeze the implementation, budget and matched training baseline before financial fitting. Report actual share-account backtests rather than just surrogate loss.

## Operating rules

For each track freeze exact grid, inputs, metric, clocks, costs, test contracts and output paths in a committed protocol before outcomes. Preserve all tried variants and failures; distinguish prior studies from new trials and record total trial counts. Use the same 3% hurdle as source, net of modeled costs, and show Sharpe, CAGR, max drawdown, turnover, yearly returns, and original-five/new-five/ten-seed results. Seeds reuse the same market history. Highest observed result and seed distribution are descriptive, not expected performance. Validate source baseline before ranking any new variant; independently recompute aggregate metrics and compare account paths where feasible.

The strict original checkpoint reproduction gate remains unmet and applies to claims about original trained model internals. The user's explicit request authorizes new exploratory financial training as a separate search once its inputs, synthetic sanity checks and bounded budgets are fixed; this does not waive the old mechanism-study gate or imply prospective validation. Assess compute before launching and use fresh outputs. Keep source artifacts immutable. No financial fit is needed for the first four saved-forecast tracks.
