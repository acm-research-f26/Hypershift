### Predeclared definitions and thresholds (fixed before any number in this plan is computed)

- **D1. Residual reversal (RR), the single primary variant.** RR(t) = −n21(t) / vol_60(t).
  - n21 is the past 21-day close-to-close return (t−21 → t), minus beta_t × the equal-weight 21-day market return over eligible stocks, then sector-demeaned over eligible stocks.
  - vol_60 is the FB feature.
  - **RR5** (the same with 5 days) is reported only as a sensitivity row and is never selected.
- **D2. Nonlinear style-residual metric (NL).** Per date, OLS of the factor-neutral 5-day return on sector dummies plus quintile dummies of each of the four leak-free styles (beta, log_dvol_20, ret_60, vol_60; 4 × 4 non-reference dummies), followed by a Spearman of the prediction against the residual. **LIN** is the A2c linear version.
- **D3. Placebo rule.** A *style-only* LightGBM is trained on the neutral target with 50 rounds and FB parameters, using as inputs only cs_z of the four leak-free styles plus sector one-hot.
  - If its pooled score on a metric has a 95% CI that excludes 0, that metric **leaks style information**.
  - BC must then use a metric whose placebo CI includes 0. If both do, use NL; if neither, STOP.
- **D4. Cost test.** The book is long top 10% / short bottom 10% *within each sector* (counts rounded, minimum 1 per side, sectors with fewer than 10 eligible stocks skipped), each leg 0.5 gross.
  - It is beta-hedged with an equal-weight basket of all eligible stocks, sized to zero ex-ante beta, with hedge trades costed.
  - **Rebalance schedules:** `daily`, `weekly` (every 5 sessions) and `weekly_buffer` (enter at the top/bottom 10% of the sector, exit when outside the top/bottom 30%).
  - **Execution:** `close1` (trade at close t+1) and `open1` (trade at open t+1).
  - **Accounting:** weights drift between trades. Costs c ∈ {2, 5, 10, 20} bp per unit of one-way traded notional / NAV.
  - **"Economically promising":** net Sharpe ≥ 0.5 at c = 5 bp **and** break-even c ≥ 10 bp, for at least one weekly schedule under **open1 and close1**.
  - Residual-label P&L is never reported as attainable.
- **D5. Regime power.**
  - Per-year long-run variance (LRV, stationary bootstrap block 10) of the dev style-residual series of the model, RR, and model − RR.
  - BC MDE under LRV multipliers {1, 1.5, 2, 3}.
  - Year-level power: one-sample t across 9 year means, with the between-year SD taken from dev.

## Review Focus

1. **Quintile dummies on dates with ties or fewer than 5 distinct values.** No crash; dummies fall back to the available bins (`test_quintile_dummies_ties`).
2. **Book with a sector of fewer than 10 eligible stocks.** Skip the sector; gross stays 1 (`test_book_gross_and_beta`).
3. **open1 on a day with a missing open.** The stock is not traded that day; no NaN reaches NAV (`test_book_missing_price_no_nan`).
4. **Membership CSV tickers with class shares or dots** (BRK.B vs BRK-B). Normalise before matching; list unmatched tickers.
5. **v1-LF code run before the freeze.** It must refuse dates ≤ 2026-10-22, exactly like v1 (`test_lf_guard`).
