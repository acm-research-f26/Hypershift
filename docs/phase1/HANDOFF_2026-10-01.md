## 4. Operational lessons

- Laptop queues are launched through Task Scheduler with `scripts/queues/launch.sh <queue>`. It generates `scripts/queues/run_<queue>.cmd` (not committed) and now allows battery operation.
- Queues die on sign-out, restart, sleep, or console Ctrl+C. Runs are resumable (finished `metrics.json` is skipped); relaunch and check `results/logs/driver.log`.
- Games, Edge or Copilot holding VRAM cause a roughly 10x slowdown (7 min/epoch vs 41 s) as memory spills to shared RAM. Close them and restart the workers.
- CPU jobs: always `CUDA_VISIBLE_DEVICES=-1`, including inside helper scripts.
- Kaggle CLI: `kaggle/.venv-kaggle/Scripts/python.exe -m kaggle.cli` (wrapped by `kaggle/*.sh`). Token in `~/.kaggle/access_token`; `KAGGLE_USER=tomphamdustry`.
- Kaggle weekly GPU quota is about 30 h. Two parallel sessions burn it twice as fast: s1 + s2 is roughly 3 + 10 h.
- `git push` to origin returns 403.
- Stale flag in `kaggle/launch.sh` (uncommitted local edit) is intentionally not committed; do not rely on it.
