**Economics behind criterion 4:**
- **Gross ceiling.** The best post-2001 value-weighted long-short CAR spread is 0.36%/month (Dai et al.). Size is
  its strongest determinant, so take about 0.2%/month ≈ 2.4%/yr for S&P 500 names. The tilt moves about 20% of NAV
  from bottom to top names, capturing about 0.2 × 2.4% ≈ **0.5%/yr gross**.
- **Cost.** About 3× NAV/yr overlay turnover (above) is about 0.30%/yr at 10 bp and 0.60%/yr at 20 bp.
- **Expected net.** About **+0.15–0.2%/yr at 10 bp** (negative at 20 bp), at a tracking error of about 1.5%/yr,
  i.e. an IR of about 0.1.
- **Probability of CONTINUE** (IR ≥ 0.30 and alpha t ≥ 1, i.e. an estimated IR of about 0.37 or more, IR standard
  error about 0.37): about 16% / 28% / 64% at a true IR of 0 / 0.15 / 0.5, before the A − C and economic-bar
  conditions, which lower it further.
