## Information timeline (five levels)

| Level | What we observe | Evidence |
|---|---|---|
| 1. Press release | Not timestamped in our data. In all 9 sampled exhibits that state a date, the release date equals the 8-K's SEC filing date | Sample table above; release text read from Exhibit 99.1 |
| 2. First market reaction | d0 for pre-open and intraday releases, d0+1 for after-close releases | 77% of the largest moves in d0−3..d0+1 fall on d0 (41%) or d0+1 (36%), at 2.3–2.4× the normal size; d0−3..d0−1 are at baseline (0.9–1.0×) |
| 3. SEC acceptance | The index-page time (ET) is reliable. The submissions-JSON clock is inconsistently converted (`PRECHECKS.md` of the filing-text round), so **the clock is not used**; SEC's official `filingDate` is | Index pages vs JSON on 12 + 8 filings |
| 4. Signal window | Market-adjusted return over d0−1..d0+1, d0 = trading day of the filing date. This covers both reaction days whatever the release hour, plus one day of possible leakage | Level 2 |
| 5. Earliest feasible order | The open after d0+1, once the window's last close is known. **The strategy trades at the first monthly formation whose previous close is on or after d0+1**: 0 to about 21 trading days later (conservative) | `signals_at`: available index d0+1 ≤ F−1 |

**Delayed filings.** The rule requires an Item 2.02 8-K to be furnished within four business days. A delayed 8-K
would show its reaction *before* d0. Days d0−3..d0−1 carry no excess movement on average (0.9–1.0× baseline), so
delays are rare enough not to matter. Any delayed event still trades only after its own window, never earlier.

**Repeated filings.** Several Item 2.02 8-Ks within 5 trading days count as one event (the first one); 8-K/A is
excluded.

**Ordering check.** 98.9% of 10-Q/10-K filings are preceded by an Item 2.02 8-K within 90 days (median gap 2 days).
Earnings are therefore released before the periodic report, and the 8-K date, not the 10-Q date, is the information
date.

## Decision: **REDEFINE BEFORE OUTCOMES**

The proposed spec (`NEXT_RESEARCH_SPEC.md`) sets d0 from the acceptance clock time ("before 16:00 ET → same day").
That clock is unreliable for part of the filings. The redefinition below is documented in `PREREG.md` before any
forward return is computed:
- d0 = the trading day of SEC's filing date;
- signal window d0−1..d0+1;
- signal available after the close of d0+1;
- traded at the next monthly formation open.

No input of the score is observed after the trade, and no forward-return window overlaps the score window.
