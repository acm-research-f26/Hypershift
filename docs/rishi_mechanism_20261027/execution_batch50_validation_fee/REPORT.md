# Why the fixed-preference comparison has a large cost difference

For the ten-seed issuer-five accounts with the 20% volatility target, the changing-score model's annual arithmetic **gross accounting contribution** exceeds the fixed validation-preference account by **1.28 percentage points**, while its modeled fee drag is **1.33 points higher**. Together these yield the previously observed **-0.05-point net difference**. The corresponding 21-session-block 95% intervals are **[-5.25, +8.54] pp** for gross and **[+1.18, +1.51] pp** for the fee-drag difference. They are descriptive, pointwise intervals on repeatedly inspected history.

The source account turnover is **22.68 times annually** for the changing-score book versus **4.56** for the fixed book; their average realized exposures are **66.62% and 68.31%**. The difference in fees therefore matters to the accounting, but these are different baskets with different positions, exposures and capital paths. Gross is computed by adding actual modeled fees back to each day's net contribution; it retains the fee-affected holdings and NAV and is **not** a zero-cost trading counterfactual. The wide gross interval does not establish that changing scores help or fail to help, and the near-zero net point estimate does not establish equivalence. Validation selected the checkpoints; this is not training Hpred or a causal component ablation.

Under full exposure, the ten-seed gross accounting difference is **+7.02 pp/year**, fee drag **+2.10 pp**, and net **+4.92 pp**. All six fixed group/policy pairs, their three components and calendar partitions are in `COMPONENTS.csv` and `CALENDAR.csv`. Subperiod values annualize daily arithmetic means; they are not calendar CAGR differences. No model or account was rerun in this attribution.

The [protocol](../VALIDATION_FIXED_FEE_PROTOCOL_20261029.md) and implementation were committed as `9ee25b3` and `8e8d738` before the component results. A separate reconstruction verified all 18 component intervals and 72 calendar partitions against the saved ledgers, with maximum error 1.89e-15. This does not validate broker costs, original-machine checkpoint inference or future performance; all prior gates remain unchanged.
