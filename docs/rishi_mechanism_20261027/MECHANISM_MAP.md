# Mechanism map: what currently explains the historical results

**Status: provisional evidence map, updated October 29, 2026.** The source's saved forecasts and simulated accounts can be reproduced, and we can explain several operations that shaped their historical returns. We cannot yet identify what information the trained network learned, assign fractions of performance to individual neural components, or establish future alpha. This map consolidates existing work; it does not complete Phase D's gated checkpoint interventions.

The selected new-five risk-managed parent-view difference is spread across multiple issuers, with offsets; [the reconciled accounting](execution_batch45_overlay_trace/REPORT.md) does not distinguish learned features from selection, exposure timing, or realized market outcomes. The subgroup was highlighted after examining multiple comparisons.

## How the headline is produced

The selected Sharpe above one combines an existing rank-blended stock selection with a subsequently searched exposure rule. The 20% basket-volatility target reduces exposure when the trailing selected basket is volatile and leaves the rest in zero-interest cash. On the ten-seed issuer-limited book, historical modeled-cost Sharpe increased from **0.925 full exposure to 1.112 with the target**, while CAGR fell from **33.37% to 26.81%** and maximum drawdown improved from **-40.94% to -21.69%**. For the same original-top-five selections, Sharpe rose from **0.896 to 1.080**. These are account-policy comparisons, not proof of incremental predictive information or a hard drawdown limit. The fixed matched study reports that all 14 primary-block paired Sharpe intervals include zero. The same target also raised the historical Sharpe of several simpler books, including parent, dynamic, training-label and QQQ, while not helping all books. See [risk attribution](RISK_ATTRIBUTION_REPORT.md).

The selection itself is meaningful: each seed's scores become cross-sectional percentile ranks, ranks are averaged within each family, and the parent and dynamic family ranks receive fixed equal weight. This selects a different basket from a capital split. For ten seeds/top ten, the executable split of two standalone books had **15.36% CAGR and 0.530 Sharpe**, versus **23.92% and 0.755** for the rank blend. This difference mixes selection, turnover, exposure and costs; it is not a causal estimate of learned complementarity. See [blend report](BLEND_BOUNDARY_REPORT.md).

A newer selected historical headline is the ten-seed continuous-hedge account at **1.325 Sharpe and 24.83% CAGR**, versus the unhedged issuer-five volatility-target account at **1.112 and 26.81%**. This uses an always-on QQQ short, not successful model-driven timing. Its annualized volatility was 15.40% versus 20.63%; the post-hoc paired Sharpe interval includes zero. Forecast signals and 12 fixed account implementations have since been reconstructed locally, subject to modeled borrow/execution and without the original hedge daily-path archive. See [hedge assessment](HEDGE_STUDY_ASSESSMENT_20261029.md).

An additional [validation-to-test diagnostic](execution_batch46_validation_rank/REPORT.md) finds ten-seed blend mean stock ranks correlate 0.966–0.995 across the annual validation/test boundary. Validation selected the checkpoints and test-period means are retrospective; neither is a training-prediction identity control or a causal explanation.

The strategy's held-out ranks are persistent: between-stock variation accounts for **87.65–96.17%** of ten-seed blend eligible rank-score variation across yearly folds, and mean adjacent-origin rank correlations range **0.931–0.973**. This is consistent with stable preferences but cannot distinguish stock-indexed parameters, persistent input characteristics or repeated changing signals. In the selected original-five/20%-target account, Alphabet, NVIDIA and Broadcom together contributed **75.64% of net annual arithmetic return**; this is an accounting fraction, not a fraction of Sharpe, a causal model contribution, or a counterfactual after removing those names. See [identity report](IDENTITY_REPORT.md).

Using the fixed top-ten category accounting convention, names also selected by both standalone families contributed **15.42 percentage points of annualized arithmetic net return**, while names in neither standalone top ten contributed **-0.63 points**. Categories partition a path that was actually held; they do not measure what a retraded exclusion would earn. See [traded-category attribution](execution_batch12_boundary/REPORT.md).

## Competing explanations and current discrimination

The [liquidity-control issuer trace](execution_batch62_liquidity_trace/REPORT.md) shows Alphabet contributes +5.36 pp/year of the +5.51 pp fixed-validation minus liquidity five-issuer point difference, with substantial offsets; the ten-stock difference reverses. This is post-hoc accounting, not issuer-removal causality or a search-adjusted finding.

The [simple style account replay](execution_batch61_validation_style_accounts/REPORT.md) moves beyond target overlap. In the ten-seed risk-managed five-issuer book, liquidity-only reports 19.99% CAGR/.833 Sharpe versus 26.71%/1.080 for fixed validation; fixed-minus-liquidity net mean +5.51 pp [+0.98,+10.18] is a single unadjusted interval among 36 after extensive prior research. In full top10, liquidity reports 25.02%/.762 with -46.05% drawdown vs changing model 23.92%/.755 and -34.88%. Different baskets and policies prevent causal attribution or prospective conclusions.

The [simple-style target comparison](execution_batch60_validation_style_overlap/REPORT.md) finds a pretest liquidity-only ranking overlaps ten-seed fixed-validation issuer-five targets by 60%/40%/80%/80% across folds, while beta-only overlaps 40%/20%/60%/40%. This is target overlap under a shared-support fallback, not a performance or causality test.

The [industry-adjusted style check](execution_batch59_validation_industry/REPORT.md) retains positive liquidity and beta partial associations on classified support across all four folds. The first two folds have 19 and 18 unclassified common-support securities; this is not causal evidence or a complete industry control.

The [joint style check](execution_batch58_validation_style_partial/REPORT.md) retains positive liquidity and beta partial rank associations with fixed validation preferences in all four folds after controlling the other three measured characteristics. These remain descriptive and do not establish a causal input or return signal.

At the first test origin, the [fixed validation-preference style check](execution_batch57_validation_style/REPORT.md) finds ten-seed rank correlations of 0.334–0.481 with trailing dollar volume and 0.238–0.458 with QQQ beta across four folds. Momentum correlations are smaller and change sign. These univariate associations do not establish causal information or return contribution.

## Decision and the next discriminating evidence

The [coverage-control issuer trace](execution_batch37_coverage_trace/REPORT.md) finds that Alphabet, AMD and NVIDIA are among the largest positive contributors to E's observed paired gap, while Meta, Palo Alto Networks and Marvell offset part of it. These are post-hoc accounting contributions across different holdings, not estimates of retraded issuer exclusions or learned-feature causation.

The [training-selected issuer exclusion test](execution_batch39_training_exclusion/REPORT.md) removes the top one or three issuers chosen by a training-label rule from new targets each fold, without recomputing neural inference. E's top-ten point results do not collapse, but paired intervals are wide and this does not prove a screened strategy is better or independent of particular winners.

The [screen accounting trace](execution_batch40_exclusion_trace/REPORT.md) shows E's three-issuer screen lost 2.98 pp/year of NVIDIA contribution relative to its original account, offset by changes across other issuers; annual paired differences change sign. This is not a single-issuer causal removal estimate.

The [coverage-control factor comparison](execution_batch38_coverage_factors/REPORT.md) retains a +20.83 pp/year FF7+QQQ intercept for E minus that limited control, with a lag-5 95% interval [7.61, 34.06] pp. This retrospective, selected-history result is a different comparison from the unresolved E-minus-training-label-identity contrast and does not establish causal neural alpha.

The [saved parent-view blend comparison](execution_batch42_extended_parent/REPORT.md) swaps separately trained daily, AH-only and AH+PM parents while keeping the saved AH+PM dynamic family fixed. At ten seeds/top ten, AH+PM minus daily parent blend is -1.59 pp/year [-4.95, +1.47], and AH+PM minus AH-only is -0.24 pp [-2.33, +1.78]. Neither establishes superiority or ±2 pp equivalence; the dynamic family still uses extended-hours inputs, so this is not a daily-only system or causal channel ablation.

The [parent-view stock trace](execution_batch43_parent_trace/REPORT.md) attributes the AH+PM-minus-daily point difference to offsetting issuer exposures and a small fee difference. These are post-hoc accounting contributions, not causal channel effects or retradable issuer-removal estimates.

The [parent-view overlay comparison](execution_batch44_parent_overlay/REPORT.md) finds that the daily-parent blend also exceeds a historical Sharpe of one under both headline 20%-target configurations. Paired headline AH+PM-versus-daily differences have wide intervals; several new-five pointwise comparisons differ, but share the same inspected market history and are not search-adjusted confirmation.

The [local precision diagnostic](execution_batch41_logmap_precision/REPORT.md) quantitatively locates one source of numerical sensitivity: a tiny CPU/GPU difference near the hyperbolic boundary is amplified by the final map. This is not an economic-signal explanation and does not pass the checkpoint reproduction gate.

The appropriate conclusion is **DEFER a learned-signal claim and further financial retraining** under the existing gates. The saved-output primary contrasts did not pass the frozen two-contrast Holm rule; the approximate economic-equivalence margin is also not established. Simulated two-year power to reject both contrasts for a +2 pp/year increment is only **2.32%**, conditional on the historical bootstrap assumptions, so a short prospective test would be poorly equipped to settle that increment. This is a resource decision, not proof that the model has no predictive information.

The decisive missing evidence is: (1) original-device layer traces and strict selected-checkpoint numerical reproduction, retaining the existing tolerance; (2) once that passes, training-only Hpred and changing-score controls, then prespecified channel/peer interventions and donor support checks; (3) independently verified publication timing, security/issuer and membership vintages, including original raw intraday inputs where available; and (4) a separately authorized candidate freeze and adequately powered prospective test if the mechanism and operational gates justify one.
