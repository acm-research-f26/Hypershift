# Assessment of the newly published hedge study

The reported Sharpe improvement is associated with an **always-on, volatility-sized QQQ short**, not a successful model-timing signal. In the ten-seed account, the source reports Sharpe 1.325 and CAGR 24.83% for the continuous hedge, versus 1.112 and 26.81% for the unhedged issuer-limited volatility-target account. Annualized volatility falls from 20.63% to 15.40%; the average short exposure is 30.03% of equity. These are source-reported, cost-and-assumed-borrow-inclusive historical account statistics, not an independently replayed account or a prospective result.

The published decision table contains 603 decisions: 201 for each of three overlapping seed groups. A separate reconstruction from the saved seed forecasts, label masks, validation outcomes and previously audited basket targets verifies all 24 calibration values, all 603 rows of numeric signal summaries and all 4,824 flags, with maximum absolute error 8.88e-16. Every raw-forecast trigger remained off. All four calibrated rules trigger on exactly the 50 decisions in 2023 and nowhere else. The 48 raw-trigger account metric rows match their corresponding unhedged baseline on the five checked metrics. This independently reconstructs signal arithmetic from saved forecasts but does not reproduce checkpoint inference or daily account paths. The source auditor reports its own reconstruction and 42 account replays.

The first reconstruction attempt failed its 1e-12 value check: it multiplied float32 labels by 100 before casting, whereas the frozen source casts to float64 first. Correcting this arithmetic order made the strict check pass; the signal flags had already matched. No source input or result was modified.

An additional local check reconstructs four existing recipes (baseline, continuous hedge, QQQ MA200 hedge and calibrated-mean hedge) across all three seed groups. Twelve target schedules match the published long/short aggregates; the source share engine and separate value-position engine agree on daily returns within 8.88e-16, and independently recomputed aggregate metrics match the publication within 1.07e-14. This uses the same modeled execution assumptions and already-audited stock allocations. The original hedge daily path archive was not published in the local checkout, so this is not a direct day-by-day comparison with the original saved paths. Run `python -m hypershift run rishi_hedge_account_audit` to repeat it.

The source's frozen own-model family contains 120 contrasts; none of its 20-session-block simultaneous active-return intervals has a positive lower bound. The continuous hedge is a descriptive control outside that family. In a separately reported post-hoc control family, the ten-seed continuous-minus-baseline annual mean is **-2.52 percentage points**, with simultaneous interval **[-12.28, +7.23] pp**; its pointwise Sharpe-difference interval **[-0.192, +0.613]** includes zero. These intervals are conditional on an extensively inspected historical period and do not correct the full earlier search. They do not show that the hedge improves future risk-adjusted performance or that the model learned a market-timing signal.

The study assumes 3% annual borrow in its base case, zero short-proceeds rebate and idle-cash yield, and modeled trading costs. Actual locate availability, recalls, margin, tax and full impact are not established. Different seed groups share market history. The risk-control and trained-network questions remain separate; this result does not close the numerical checkpoint, feature-intervention, point-in-time publication or prospective confirmation gates. No candidate was activated.
