Frozen before computing this diagnostic, after earlier results on these years have been widely inspected. For each annual fold 2022–2025, original-five/new-five/ten-seed group and parent/dynamic/blend family (36 fixed cells), use only published validation and test predictions and original eligibility masks. Apply the source's per-seed within-date average-tie percentile ranks, average ranks within family and fixed equal parent/dynamic blend. Per security average eligible ranks separately over validation and test; require at least five eligible dates in each period. Report support counts, Spearman correlation and median absolute rank difference over matched security IDs. Equal-date means are descriptive; the 36 cells and overlapping seeds are not independent market replications. Record all cells including undefined correlations, and validate prediction/axis shapes and chronology. Do not use test labels, fit or select model parameters, change accounts, run checkpoint inference, or call the validation mean Hpred: these validation data were used for checkpoint selection and are not training predictions. This diagnostic cannot establish causation or translate rank persistence into performance. No new protected data or prospective action; original numerical gate unchanged.
