# Daily source reconstruction

The newly published Yahoo, Tiingo and provisional membership files reproduce the assembled daily inputs across **156 securities and 2,011 dates**, using the saved alias, exclusion and four acquisition-settlement rules. Raw close, adjusted economic close, dollar volume, membership, tradability, and SPY/QQQ adjusted close and dollar volume all match the published panels within `1e-12` relative and `1e-8` absolute tolerance. Membership and tradability match exactly. Maximum absolute differences are 2.85e-14 for prices and 1.53e-5 dollars for dollar volumes; the latter reflects floating-point serialization at large dollar values, and passes the stated relative tolerance. Each of the four settlement factors and consideration values also matches the manifest.

The independent in-memory reconstruction is `daily_raw_reconstruction_20261029.py`; it reads Tiingo JSON and Yahoo CSV, not the Yahoo pickle, and leaves original files untouched. Previous supplement verification checks all 2,660 published hashes. This establishes that the assembled panels follow their recorded sources and assumptions; it does **not** prove historical membership or symbol identity was correct, that Yahoo/Tiingo values were available at the time, that retrospective adjustments are point-in-time, or that assumed completion-close settlements reproduce broker execution. Membership remains a provisional third-party reconstruction and earlier unpriced merger outcomes remain missing under the recorded rules. No result or model was refitted or rescored.

Subsequent source-chain check: `membership_source_check_20261029.py` independently reads and hashes the 11 published third-party annual YAMLs, checks all ten year-end closures, and reconstructs all 53 recorded events and 11,039 constituent rows in 108 monthly snapshots exactly. It preserves ticker `ON` as text rather than a YAML boolean. This verifies the monthly table follows the published source; continuity and internal consistency do not establish that the source is historically correct or was available before trading.
