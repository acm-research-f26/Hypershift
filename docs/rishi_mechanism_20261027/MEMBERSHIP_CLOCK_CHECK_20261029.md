# Membership changes at the decision boundary

The saved eligibility mask uses the monthly membership snapshot for the **entry session**, while price/tradability history ends at the preceding exchange-session close. The model decision is recorded at 09:20 Eastern on entry day. Historical announcement/publication timestamps for the membership source are not established, so this convention is not itself proof of look-ahead; it is an unresolved availability question.

The 201 test origins contain **seven** prior-close-to-entry membership transitions, involving **27 security-change rows**. Thirteen entering securities are eligible under the original mask. None receives positive target weight in the checked original-five, new-five or ten-seed AH+PM-parent/dynamic **issuer-five** allocations. The 2021-12-31 to 2022-01-03 transition contributes 13 of those change rows; the other transitions occur at the February/December 2022, July/August 2023, July 2024 and June 2025 entry-month boundaries. `python -m hypershift run rishi_membership_clock_audit` lists every security and eligible status.

Zero selected entrants is **not** a zero-impact result. Excluded leavers, extra eligible peers and altered cross-sectional rank denominators can affect forecasts or selection even when an entrant is not purchased. This check does not rerun models under prior-close membership, verify announcements, assess top-ten allocations or quantify account impact. Trained-checkpoint counterfactuals remain behind the unchanged reproduction gate. Public first-announcement times and identity mapping need separate evidence before an availability conclusion; no training, protected scoring or prospective action occurred.

## Dated-source follow-up

The eight source events underlying these seven transitions are **all recorded as effective before the preceding-close origin**: 2021-12-20, 2022-01-24, 2022-11-21, 2023-06-07, 2023-06-20, 2023-07-17, 2024-06-24 and 2025-05-19. The difference arises because the study applies a monthly snapshot and carries the prior month's membership until the next month; it does not imply the event happened overnight at the decision boundary. This is a retrospective comparison to the recorded effective dates, not proof that every original data feed was available then.

Nasdaq primary announcements independently corroborate seven of the eight events well before the associated decision: [2021 annual additions](https://ir.nasdaq.com/node/104321), [ODFL/PTON](https://ir.nasdaq.com/node/104391/pdf), [ENPH/OKTA](https://www.globenewswire.com/news-release/2022/11/12/2554432/0/en/Enphase-Energy-Inc-to-Join-the-NASDAQ-100-Index-Beginning-November-21-2022.html), [GEHC/FISV](https://ir.nasdaq.com/news-releases/news-release-details/ge-healthcare-technologies-inc-join-nasdaq-100-indexr-beginning), [TTD/ATVI](https://ir.nasdaq.com/news-releases/news-release-details/trade-desk-inc-join-nasdaq-100-index-beginning-july-17th-2023), [ARM/SIRI](https://ir.nasdaq.com/news-releases/news-release-details/arm-holdings-plc-join-nasdaq-100-indexr-beginning-june-24-2024) and [SHOP/MDB](https://ir.nasdaq.com/node/108996).
