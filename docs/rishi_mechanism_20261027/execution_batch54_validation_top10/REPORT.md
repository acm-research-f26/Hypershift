# Fixed validation preferences on the ten-stock book

At base modeled costs, the ten-seed changing-score top-ten account reports **23.92% CAGR and 0.755 Sharpe**, versus **17.27% and 0.597** for the fixed validation-preference account. The paired annual arithmetic net-mean difference is **+6.17 percentage points**, with a 21-session-block 95% interval **[-2.81, +16.11] pp**. At twice modeled costs, the difference is **+4.56 pp [-4.39, +14.48]**. These intervals include zero and extend beyond the ±2 pp margin; the comparison establishes neither superiority nor equivalence.

The fixed account trades much less: annual turnover **2.95** versus **24.91** for the model at base costs. The two accounts have different holdings and risk; their difference is not a causal estimate of changing neural information. This follow-up was selected after earlier results, uses validation forecasts from checkpoints chosen on that same validation fold, and tests repeatedly inspected 2022–2025 history. It is not the training Hpred control or a replacement for the originally frozen primary mechanism family. The three seed groups overlap and share one market history.
