# Industry-matched activity and coverage control

The saved **six-channel linear activity/coverage control does not reproduce the blend's historical performance**, even when it inherits the blend's observed industry budgets, position counts and position-size distribution. This makes the simple control a weak explanation of the blend's result, but it does not rule out nonlinear activity information or establish which features the neural models learned.

This exploratory follow-up was frozen after the earlier coverage findings: protocol ccf39f7 and code b49683d. It uses the existing training-fold-fitted `coverage6` score without refitting. At every decision the control keeps each original account's FF12/unknown group budget and positive-weight multiset, then chooses stocks within each group by the saved six-channel coverage/activity score and the fixed tie priority. The group schedule therefore comes from the original model, and the control is not a standalone industry-only strategy.

The fixed block-5 and block-63 intervals also lie above zero in both comparisons; all six intervals are in `PAIRED_CONTRASTS.csv`. These are pointwise, selected-history diagnostics after extensive previous inspection, not a new confirmatory family or a replacement for the failed primary E-minus-E_Hlabel gate. Different holdings, realized market risk and execution mean the difference is not a causal decomposition of activity features or learned peer information. The E control shares an average **2.34 names and 23.33% target weight** with E. Its annualized turnover is **62.04×**, versus approximately **24.91×** for E; fees and turnover therefore materially differ. The E_Hlabel control turnover is 64.67×. All actual account metrics, costs, exposures, annual returns, group constraints and overlaps are retained.

The source's six channels are after-hours and premarket coverage, first-trade delay and last-trade age. The original score-fallback receipts show zero missing eligible scores in each fold. This does **not** mean all underlying input fields were observed: the source feature scaler replaces missing standardized values with zero before fitting the existing control. SEC-derived FF12 groups are incomplete and may become stale; price-series identity and first provider publication remain unverified. The simple linear control has limited capacity and is not a matched neural feature ablation.
